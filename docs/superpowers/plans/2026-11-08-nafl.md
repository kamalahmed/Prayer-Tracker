# Phase 15 — Nafl implementation plan

Goal: a third Streaks tab for optional worship with local-only logging and Quran reminders.
Architecture: Room v3 stores stable item definitions, per-day logs and reminder times. Pure statistics aggregate immutable snapshots; Hilt ViewModel serializes edits and exposes failures. Compose reuses dashboard components. AlarmManager schedules one-shot local-time reminders and reschedules on receive, boot, time changes and upgrade. Notification channels separate silent/default reminders from optional alarm sound. All reminders are off until explicitly added/enabled.

Statistics semantics: baseline Sunnah is always the five named slots totaling 12 (optional slots never inflate that total). A log count >0 is a completed day; Quran pages/minutes never implicitly log completion. Rates use elapsed days in the selected calendar period, limited by first recorded Nafl activity (untracked dates remain blank). Streaks end today or yesterday. Disabled items retain history and remain available in reports. Renaming preserves IDs and history. Future dates cannot be logged. Calendar intensity measures completed worship items, with accessible raw counts.
